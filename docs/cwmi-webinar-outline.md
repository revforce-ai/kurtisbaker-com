# CWMI — "Freedom Ready" Workshop / Perfect Webinar Outline

**Purpose:** the one-to-many top-of-funnel accelerator (rollout §5). Does the
**Realization** at scale, then offers the **Freedom Readiness Audit** (or, for
hot prospects, a direct Blueprint call).
**Format:** ~60–75 min live (then evergreen replay). Webinar, or in-person
owner workshop / lunch-and-learn.
**Frameworks:** Russell Brunson "Perfect Webinar" (Big Domino → 3 Secrets →
stack/offer) + McCall Jones REP.

> ⚠️ NJ state-registered RIA, building to SEC standard. **Educational, not
> advice. No return/performance claims.** Any case examples need testimonial/
> conflict disclosures. Disclaimers on the registration page and verbally.
> Counsel reviews the deck + script.

## Structure

### A. Intro & the Big Domino (8 min)
- Hook + credentials (CFP®, CEPA®, AIF®; *Master Your Finances*) — fast.
- **The one belief that makes everything else click (the Big Domino):**
  > "If I can make you believe that *freedom — not sale price — is the real
  goal, and that it must be engineered years before you exit*, then everything
  else about how you run and eventually leave your business changes."
- Promise the 3 Secrets + "stay to the end for [the Audit]."

*REP — Realization framing for the whole session.*

### B. The stakes / why now (7 min)
- The trapped-wealth reality: most of an owner's net worth in one illiquid asset.
- **The hard stat:** most businesses that go to market never sell. (Insert the
  sourced figure from the competitive benchmark — commonly cited that only
  ~20–30% of listed businesses actually sell. Verify before stating.)
- "Hope is not an exit plan. The market won't wait for you to be ready."

### C. Secret #1 — Realization (12 min)
**Belief to install:** *Your number isn't your valuation — it's the personal,
after-tax wealth that lets you walk away for good.*
- Break the false belief: "I'll just sell for $X and be fine."
- Teach the two-readiness model (business ready vs. owner ready).
- Mini-story / illustrative example (compliant, disclosed).

### D. Secret #2 — Effort (12 min)
**Belief to install:** *A business that depends on you is worth less — value is
built by making it run (and transfer) without you.*
- Teach value drivers / owner-dependence at a high level (the value-acceleration
  idea — attribute to the Exit Planning Institute methodology).
- "This is work, but it's *leveraged* work — and it's the difference between a
  business and a sellable asset."

### E. Secret #3 — Proof / coordination (12 min)
**Belief to install:** *Four disconnected advisors leave money on the table; one
fiduciary coordinating the whole board changes the outcome.*
- Why silos cost money (tax + estate + deal misalignment).
- The fiduciary standard; credentials are verifiable (cfp.net, EPI, Fi360).

*REP across C–E maps cleanly: Secret 1 = Realization, Secret 2 = Effort,
Secret 3 = Proof.*

### F. Transition to the offer (3 min)
> "So you see the gap and the path. The question is whether you map *your* gap
> alone — or with someone who does this every day. Here's how to do that with
> me, at no cost…"

### G. The offer — the Freedom Readiness Audit (8 min)
- Present the Audit value stack (landing-page §3 / offer-stack §4): Scorecard,
  Wealth-at-Risk, Exit Options Map, One Big Gap — **$2,500 value, $0**.
- **Honest scarcity:** limited Audits per month.
- **Clarity Guarantee** restated (experience only).
- **CTA:** book the Audit now (live link + QR for in-person). Hot prospects who
  pre-qualify (revenue/timeline) → invite straight to a Blueprint call.

### H. Q&A + close (10 min)
- Answer live; every answer ends pointing back to the Audit CTA.
- Final: "The best time to plan your exit was five years ago. The second best
  time is right now — book your Audit before you close this tab."
