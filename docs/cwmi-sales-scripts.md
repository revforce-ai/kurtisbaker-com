# CWMI — Two-Call Sales Scripts

**For:** the two-call close in `docs/cwmi-offer-stack-rollout.md` §5.
**Call 1:** Freedom Readiness Audit (diagnose, land the Realization).
**Call 2:** Blueprint Present (prescribe, run the stack slide, close).
**Frameworks:** Brunson stack slide + McCall Jones REP (Realization · Effort ·
Proof). These are guides, not word-for-word reads — stay conversational.

> ⚠️ NJ state-registered RIA (building to SEC standard). **Never promise returns
> or performance.** Guarantee the *experience* only (Clarity Guarantee). Don't
> give specific investment/tax/legal advice on these calls — diagnose and
> prescribe the engagement. Counsel reviews scripts.

---

## CALL 1 — The Freedom Readiness Audit (60 min)

**Objective:** make the readiness gap undeniable and earn the Blueprint call.
**NOT the objective:** pitch the engagement. Selling here kills trust.

### Pre-call (5 min, before they join)
Review intake: revenue band, years-to-exit, "biggest reason you're here." Have
their numbers/scorecard ready so it feels prepared, not generic.

### 1. Frame & permission (3 min)
> "Thanks for making time. Here's how I'd like to use our hour: I'll walk you
> through where your business and your personal finances actually stand on
> readiness — two different questions most owners never separate. You'll leave
> with your scorecard and the single biggest move to make this year, whether or
> not we ever work together. Sound fair? … Great. Be straight with me and I'll
> be straight with you."

*REP — sets up Realization; lowers sales resistance immediately.*

### 2. Discovery — their story (15 min)
Open-ended, listen more than talk. Take notes for the stack slide later.
- "Tell me about the business — what you built, how long, where it is today."
- "If you took a real 60-day vacation, what breaks?"
- "What does life look like the day after you no longer own this?"
- "Have you ever had the business formally valued? What's your number in your
  head — and where'd it come from?"
- "Who's on your team today — CPA, attorney, advisor? Do they talk to each
  other?"
- **The pain question:** "What worries you most when you think about eventually
  stepping back or selling?"

*REP — Realization: their own words become the gap you reflect back.*

### 3. The two scorecards (20 min)
Walk **business readiness** then **personal readiness**, live.
- **Business:** value drivers, owner-dependence, customer concentration,
  transferability. "A business that needs you is worth less to everyone but
  you — here's where yours sits."
- **Personal:** "Even at a great sale price — net of tax — does the number fund
  the life you described? Let's see." (Wealth-at-Risk: % of net worth in one
  illiquid asset.)
- **Exit Options Map:** "Here are the realistic paths for a company your size —
  third-party sale, internal/management transfer, family. Each has a different
  timeline and tax outcome."

*REP — Effort: the rigor of the scorecards IS the value; Proof: framed via the
CEPA/value-acceleration lens buyers actually use.*

### 4. The One Big Gap (7 min)
> "If you do one thing this year, it's this: [the gap]. Here's why it's the
> highest-leverage move and what it's worth to close it."

Let it land. Silence is fine.

### 5. The bridge to Call 2 (10 min) — earn it, don't pitch
> "So that's the diagnosis. The question is whether you want to keep flying solo
> on this or have one fiduciary own the whole picture with you. I put together
> what a complete plan would look like for a situation like yours — the
> business value work, the personal plan, the tax and estate coordination, the
> exit sequencing. I'd rather walk you through it properly than rush it now.
> Can we grab 60–90 minutes next week? I'll show you exactly what it includes
> and what it costs — no pressure, you decide after."

**If yes →** book the Blueprint live, on the call. Confirm both calendars.
**If "send me info" →** "I'll send your scorecard today. The plan itself only
makes sense walked through live — that's the next call. Let's hold a time so it
doesn't drift." (Book it.)

### Post-call
Send leave-behind (scorecard + One Big Gap memo) via GHL W5. Move to Blueprint
Booked or Nurture.

## CALL 2 — The Blueprint Present (60–90 min)

**Objective:** present the Freedom Ready Wealth Blueprint, run the stack slide,
handle objections, close (or set a clear next step).

### 1. Recap & re-land the gap (5 min)
> "Last time we found [their number / their gap / their wealth-at-risk]. Has
> anything changed how you're thinking about it since? … Today I'll show you
> exactly how we'd close that gap, what it includes, and what it costs."

*REP — Realization re-anchored before value.*

### 2. Present the stack (25–30 min) — one layer at a time, REP each
Walk the six layers from offer stack §5. For **each**: name it → the Realization
it solves → the Effort/depth behind it → the Proof. Don't rush; let value
accumulate.
1. Comprehensive Financial Plan → "your real number, net of tax"
2. Fiduciary Investment Management → "diversify wealth *outside* the business"
3. Business Value Acceleration → "make it run — and sell — without you"
4. Exit & Transition Strategy → "engineered early, not under pressure"
5. Tax & Estate Coordination → "one quarterback, not four silos"
6. Quarterly Freedom Reviews → "freedom is maintained, not achieved"

Then bonuses (each kills an objection): FRB Scorecard, Heirs/Spouse session,
Life-After-Exit plan, Master Your Finances network.

### 3. The stack slide + anchor (5 min)
> "If you assembled this piece by piece — a CFP®, a CEPA®, an AIF®, plus a tax
> and estate quarterback — you'd be looking at about **$42,200 in the first year
> alone**, across four people who don't talk to each other."

Pause on the total before price.

### 4. Price reveal (5 min)
> "You're not paying that. Your engagement is **[CWMI's actual disclosed fee —
> AUM % and/or flat planning fee]**, with one fiduciary accountable for the
> whole outcome."

State the real, ADV-disclosed fee plainly. No fake discount off the "value."

### 5. Risk reversal (3 min)
> "And here's my commitment — the Clarity Guarantee: complete the planning and
> if you don't have more clarity on your readiness and your next move than ever
> before, you're under no obligation to continue."

*Compliance: experience only — no return/outcome guarantee.*

### 6. Close (5 min) — assume the next step
> "I think the cleanest path is to get started so we can [close the One Big Gap].
> Shall we get the paperwork going?"
Then silence. Let them answer.

### 7. Onboarding (if yes)
Trigger GHL W8: deliver **Form ADV (2A/2B)**, engagement letter / e-sign,
schedule kickoff. The regulated CWMI relationship begins here.

## Objection handling (REP-based)

| Objection | Response (Realization / Effort / Proof) |
|---|---|
| **"It's too expensive."** | Reframe to cost of inaction: "Compared to what? Most owners leave six or seven figures on the table on a poorly planned exit, and overpay tax on top. The fee is a fraction of one avoidable mistake." (R) |
| **"I already have an advisor / CPA."** | "Good — keep them. The gap isn't competence, it's that nobody's coordinating them around *your* exit. That's the job I'd own." (E) |
| **"Let me think about it."** | "Totally fair — what specifically do you want to think through? Let's make sure I've answered it." (Surface the real objection; don't let it walk.) |
| **"I'm not ready to sell yet."** | "Perfect — that's exactly when this works. The best exits are engineered years early. Waiting is the expensive choice." (R) |
| **"How do I know this works?"** | "Verify my credentials yourself — CFP Board, the Exit Planning Institute, Fi360. And the first step is low-risk by design, backed by the Clarity Guarantee." (P) |
| **"I need to talk to my spouse."** | "Absolutely — this affects you both. Let's get them on a short call; there's a session built in for exactly that." (Bonus 2.) |
| **"Just send me the proposal."** | "I'll send the recap. The plan's worth walking through so you can ask questions in real time — got 20 minutes Thursday?" |

> **Compliance guardrail for all objections:** never rebut with a
> return/performance claim. Use cost-of-inaction, coordination, fiduciary
> standard, and verifiable credentials — not "we'll make you X%."

## Tonal rules (McCall Jones)
- **Realization before pitch** — never present the offer before the gap is felt.
- **Charisma = certainty + warmth** — speak plainly, hold silence, don't
  over-explain or apologize for the fee.
- **Effort visible, owner's effort minimal** — "we do the heavy lifting."
- **Proof, not promises** — credentials and process, never returns.
