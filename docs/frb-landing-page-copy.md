**H2: You built something most people never will. So why doesn't it feel like freedom?**

You're successful on paper. But if you're honest:

- **Your wealth is trapped.** Most of your net worth is locked inside one
  illiquid asset you can't easily sell.
- **The business needs you.** Take a real 60-day vacation and what happens? You
  own a high-paying job, not a sellable asset.
- **Nobody sees the whole board.** Your CPA, your attorney, your investment guy
  — all in separate silos, none accountable for *your* outcome.

find out they weren't ready only after they've left money — sometimes
seven figures of it — on the table.
