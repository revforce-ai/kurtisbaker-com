## Category 1 — Exit-planning / CEPA practices (DIRECT COMPS, verified)

### 1A. Exit Factor — exitfactor.com
National franchise (United Franchise Group), founder Jessica Fialkovich (CEPA,
EPI-listed), 100+ U.S. territories. The scaled, productized player.
- **Lead magnet:** two-tier free hook — instant **Business Valuation Calculator**
  → deeper **Exit Assessment**; plus a best-selling book as authority bait.
- **Funnel/CTA:** land → free tool → human call. CTAs: *"Schedule a Free
  Consultation"* / *"Find Your Local Expert."* Localized pages route to a nearby
  franchisee. Published **"Proven 5-Step Process."**
- **Proof:** founder credibility (1,500+ exits, author, keynote), franchisee
  testimonials, per-office team pages.
- **Pricing:** none for clients (all "free consultation"); only the *franchise*
  side discloses (~$100K to buy a territory).
- **SEO:** strong programmatic — industry × city landing pages (restaurants,
  plumbing, gyms × Austin, Atlanta, Naperville).
- **STEAL:** the two-tier free magnet (calculator → assessment → call); a named,
  repeatable process as a trust device; industry×geo programmatic SEO.
  **LEAVE:** franchise model + "contact us" pricing wall — a boutique can
  out-position on transparency + specialization, not geographic footprint.

### 1B. Certified Exit Planners — certifiedexitplanners.com
Boutique exit planning + brokerage + valuation (Tega Cay, SC). Tight single-CTA
funnel.
- **Lead magnet:** free **"Business Wellness Check-Up"** — 3-minute assessment,
  *"no commitment or sales call required"*; secondary gated **Exit Readiness
  Checklist PDF** (go.certifiedexitplanners.com).
- **Funnel/CTA:** one CTA repeated everywhere — the 3-minute check-up. Reframe:
  *"most owners are 2–3 years away… see where you stand today, no obligation."*
- **Proof:** CEPA in the brand name; named contact + address; brokerage depth
  (can take you through the actual sale).
- **Pricing:** none published.
- **SEO:** trade-specific long tail (lawn care, tree service, "green
  businesses").
- **STEAL:** the single ultra-low-friction CTA used site-wide (conversion
  discipline); the **"you're 2–3 years out" reframe** that captures owners
  *before* they're ready (long nurture window); gated checklist as a 2nd capture.
  **LEAVE:** the trades they already own in SEO — pick adjacent verticals.

### 1C. Beyond Your Exit Wealth Management — beyondyourexitwm.com
Fee-only RIA (SEC IAPD #315874, NAPFA), founder Tom Poltersdorf Jr., **CFP® +
CEPA**. The advisor-led model — **the closest analog to CWMI** — and the
strongest authority + pricing-transparency play.
- **Lead magnet:** **Free Readiness Assessment** (scores buyer-attractiveness,
  timing, and whether a sale funds the owner's future) → personalized report.
  Productized paid entry: **"Engagement 1: Financial Freedom Blueprint"**
  (~3-month plan as a low-commitment trial).
- **Funnel/CTA:** soft/consultative — **15-min complimentary intro call** →
  Readiness Assessment → paid Blueprint → AUM relationship.
- **Proof:** CFP® + CEPA, fee-only/NAPFA/fiduciary, co-author of *EXIT SMART*,
  cites EPI data, award-winning site.
- **Pricing:** **most transparent of the three** — a "Services & Fees" page +
  public ADV 2A; structure disclosed (**half at signing, half at plan
  presentation**, quarterly reviews).
- **SEO/content:** deepest engine — **podcast "Business Exit Success,"** blog,
  categorized **"Knowledge Center."**
- **STEAL:** fee-transparency posture; the **productized "Blueprint" trial** that
  converts assessment leads into paid relationships (this validates CWMI's exact
  two-call → Blueprint model); podcast + Knowledge Center as a moat.
  **LEAVE:** don't clone his multi-year podcast back-catalog — differentiate on
  format (video, calculators, tools).

> ⚠️ **Direct-competitor alert:** Beyond Your Exit is doing almost exactly what
> CWMI plans (CFP+CEPA, free readiness assessment → productized Blueprint →
> AUM). CWMI's edge must be sharper: the **"Freedom"** brand + Kurt's media
> platform (*Master Your Finances*, speaking) + the AIF® third credential +
> the **life-after-exit / identity** layer none of them sell.

## Category 2 — Owner-focused wealth firms / RIAs (positioning)
Most RIAs targeting business owners still lead with generic "comprehensive
wealth management" and hide everything behind "Schedule a Consultation."
Beyond Your Exit (1C) is the exception that proves owner-niche + transparency
wins. **Pattern to beat:** vague "we serve business owners" with no owner-
specific lead magnet. **STEAL from the best:** a *named* owner-specific
diagnostic (not a generic "free consultation"). **CWMI lane:** own
"business-owner exit + wealth" as one integrated promise, not two services.
*(Verify specific firm pages before external citation.)*

## Category 3 — M&A advisory / business brokers (exit-intent capture)
Marketplaces and broker networks (e.g., **BizBuySell** as the dominant listings
marketplace; regional/national broker franchises) capture owners at the
*transaction* moment with valuation tools and "what's my business worth?"
hooks. **STEAL:** the "what's my business worth?" instant-valuation hook is the
highest-intent magnet in the space (Exit Factor borrowed it too). **LEAVE:**
the transactional, list-it-and-sell-it framing — CWMI's wedge is engineering
value *years before* the listing, which brokers don't do. **Partner, don't
fight:** brokers are a referral source, not just a competitor.
*(Confirm current tool/CTA specifics before citing.)*

## Category 4 — Brunson-style funnels (mechanics to borrow)
Russell Brunson / ClickFunnels (clickfunnels.com) and the broader info-product
world prove the mechanics CWMI is adopting: a single-CTA value-stack page, the
"Perfect Webinar" (Big Domino → 3 Secrets → stack → close), free-plus-shipping /
free-assessment front ends, and relentless follow-up. **STEAL:** funnel
discipline (one offer, one CTA), the stack slide, the webinar structure (already
in our outline). **LEAVE:** the hypey, scarcity-timer, "guru" tone — fatal for a
fiduciary. **Translate, don't transplant:** Brunson mechanics + RIA-compliant
restraint.

## Category 5 — Counter-example (what NOT to do)
The archetype to avoid: the generic advisor/firm site — stock photos of
sailboats and golf, "comprehensive solutions for your financial future," a
brochure of services, no lead magnet, no proof beyond a logo, a single buried
"Contact Us," zero content engine, and no owner-specific message. It ranks for
nothing, captures no leads, and is indistinguishable from every other firm.
**Lesson:** specificity (business owners), a real diagnostic magnet (the Audit),
verifiable proof (credentials with links), and a content engine are exactly what
separates the leaders above from this.

## Synthesized pattern map — what the best do

1. **Lead with a free, fast, low-friction diagnostic** — a "readiness score" /
   "what's my business worth?" — never a brochure. (All three direct comps.)
2. **One dominant, repeated CTA**; no nav rabbit holes. (Certified Exit Planners.)
3. **The "2–3 years out" reframe** captures owners *before* they're ready —
   long nurture window. (Highest-leverage borrowed idea.)
4. **CEPA is table stakes; pair it with a 2nd trust marker** (CFP®/fiduciary,
   deal count, book/podcast). CWMI has CFP®+CEPA+**AIF®** — a credential edge.
5. **A named, repeatable process** as a trust device (Exit Factor's "5 Steps,"
   Beyond Your Exit's "Blueprint"). CWMI's = the Freedom Ready Wealth Blueprint.
6. **A content/authority moat** (podcast, knowledge center, programmatic SEO).
   CWMI already has *Master Your Finances* + speaking — underused as a funnel.
7. **Productized trial engagement** converts assessment → paid without a big
   commitment. (Validates CWMI's Audit → Blueprint two-call model.)
8. **Pricing transparency is the open lane** — almost everyone hides it; the one
   firm that shows fee *structure* stands out for it.

## Gaps CWMI can own (the wedge)

- **"Freedom," not "exit."** Competitors sell a transaction; CWMI sells the life
  the transaction funds — a more emotional, ownable brand.
- **The 3rd credential + media platform.** CFP®+CEPA+**AIF®** plus *Master Your
  Finances* + Kurtis Baker Speaks = authority none of the comps match.
- **Life-after-exit / identity planning.** Nobody sells the "who am I after I
  sell?" layer — CWMI's Bonus 3. Real differentiation.
- **Pricing-structure transparency** (mirror Beyond Your Exit, beat the rest).
- **Un-owned verticals** for SEO (professional services, healthcare practices,
  light manufacturing) instead of fighting owned trades/geos.
- **Integrated wealth + exit under one fiduciary** — most are *either* brokers
  *or* planners; CWMI is the coordinated whole.
