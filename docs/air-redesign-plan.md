# Attitudes In Reverse® (air.ngo) — Website Redesign & Sustainability Plan

Board-prep strategy for transforming air.ngo into the best-in-class youth mental-health and suicide-prevention website in the country — rolled out in three tiers, anchored to a plan that makes AIR a sustainable, growing organization.

> **Companion documents**
> - `docs/air-qc-report.md` — quality-control audit of the *current* air.ngo site
> - `/air-sandbox` — a working design sandbox (deploys to a temporary preview link); see "How to view the sandbox" below

---

## North Star

**Hopeful, credible, urgent — without ever being heavy.**

AIR sits at the intersection of grief and hope. The site has to do something almost no other site has to: hold a life-or-death subject *and* feel safe, warm, and motivating to a 15-year-old, a worried parent, a school principal with a budget, and a donor — often on the same page.

The tone target: **the calm authority of a children's hospital + the warmth of a story you'd tell a friend + the momentum of a movement.**

Design principles that flow from that:

1. **Help is never more than one glance away.** A crisis line (988) is visible on every screen. This is both a moral obligation and a best-in-class signal.
2. **Hope leads, loss supports.** Kenny's story is the engine, but the hero sells the future, not the tragedy.
3. **Calm, breathable, light.** Teal + warm coral, generous whitespace, soft motion. Never clinical, never dark, never alarmist.
4. **One clear next step per audience.** Students → "get help / start a club." Schools → "book a presentation." Donors → "give." Volunteers → "get involved." Never make a visitor guess.
5. **Evidence on the surface.** "Research-validated by TCNJ" does more for a school district than any adjective.

## Who the site serves (and what each audience needs)

A nonprofit site fails when it's built for the board instead of the visitor. AIR has **five** distinct audiences. The architecture must serve all five without diluting any:

| Audience | What they're thinking | The one action we want | Their proof point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Students / youth** | "Is something wrong with me? Is it safe to talk?" | Find help, start a conversation, start a club | Peer stories, plain language, zero stigma |
| **Parents / caregivers** | "How do I spot it? What do I do?" | Learn the signs, take Youth MH First Aid | Practical checklists, a real human to call |
| **Educators / schools** | "Is this credible, safe, and bookable?" | Request a presentation | Research validation, testimonials, easy booking |
| **Donors / sponsors** | "Is my money creating real impact?" | Donate / sponsor a school | Impact numbers, transparency, a clear ask |
| **Partners / press** | "Are these the right people to amplify?" | Co-host, cover, refer | Credibility, media kit, contact |

**Strategic implication:** the homepage is a *router*, not a brochure. Each audience should find their lane within one scroll.

## Pattern map — what every best-in-class mission site does

1. **Crisis help visible everywhere** — non-negotiable for this category.
2. **A "get help / give help" fork** near the top.
3. **Impact in numbers, fast** — students reached, schools served, lives touched.
4. **Story-led, photography-forward** — real faces, real stakes, real hope.
5. **One frictionless donation flow** — and a *recurring* option.
6. **A participation engine** — clubs, volunteers, ambassadors — so the audience grows the org.
7. **Evidence on the surface** — research, partners, outcomes.
8. **A nurture loop** — newsletter / email as the relationship that outlives the visit.

The sandbox combines: **Jed's get-help/give-help clarity + 988's calm palette + charity: water's give-flow discipline + Active Minds' clubs engine + TWLOHA's movement voice.**

## How the website becomes the *anchor* of a sustainable AIR

The website is not a brochure — it's the **hub of a flywheel**. Every other channel (school visits, events, therapy-dog appearances, press, social) should drive to the site, and the site should convert that attention into a durable relationship.

```
        SCHOOL VISITS / EVENTS / DOGS / PRESS / SOCIAL
                          │  (drive traffic)
                          ▼
                   ┌──────────────┐
        ┌─────────▶│   air.ngo    │──────────┐
        │          │  (the hub)   │          │
        │          └──────────────┘          │
   (nurture:                              (capture:
   email, content,                       newsletter, donate,
   campaigns)                            book, volunteer)
        │                                     │
        └──────────  EMAIL / CRM  ◀───────────┘
                 (the relationship engine)
```

**The four jobs of the site:**

1. **Connect with existing clients/community** — schools that have booked, donors, volunteers, therapy-dog families. Give them a logged-out "alumni" home: re-book a presentation, renew a gift, share their story.
2. **Market outward to new schools & districts** — SEO/GEO + a credible "Bring AIR to your school" funnel so a principal in a new state can find AIR and book without a phone call.
3. **Create hooks that pull new people in** — free, genuinely useful content (a "Start the Conversation" parent guide, signs-and-symptoms toolkit, club starter kit). Value first, ask second.
4. **Nurture everyone toward deeper commitment** — newsletter + email journeys move a visitor: *reader → subscriber → donor → monthly donor → advocate.*

**Sustainability levers the site must enable:**

- **Recurring giving** ("Become a Monthly Supporter") — predictable revenue is what makes a nonprofit durable.
- **Sponsor-a-school** — a named, tangible product a donor or local business can buy ("$X sponsors a presentation for 300 students").
- **Grant-readiness** — impact data, research validation, and outcome reporting visible and exportable for grant applications.
- **Fee-for-service programs** — Youth Mental Health First Aid trainings and conference tickets, booked and paid on-site.
- **The clubs/ambassador engine** — students who start clubs become a volunteer + future-donor pipeline that compounds for years.

## Three-Tier Rollout

Ship each tier as it's ready. Tier 1 is live in days; by Tier 3 it's award-grade.

### Tier 1 — Stabilize & Polish (ship this week)

**Goal:** Fix what's broken, make it safe and credible, and stand up a modern foundation. Take air.ngo from "dated and risky" to "clean and trustworthy."

- [ ] **Crisis line on every page** — pinned 988 / Crisis Text Line bar. (Moral + best-in-class.)
- [ ] **Fix the QC issues** in `air-qc-report.md` — kill duplicate pages, retire/redirect the `host.air.ngo` legacy subdomain, settle on one canonical host (`www` *or* apex, 301 the other), fix broken links and mixed content.
- [ ] **A clear "Get Help / Get Involved" fork** in the header and hero.
- [ ] **One frictionless "Donate" button** in the nav, styled to stand out, on a reputable processor.
- [ ] **"Bring AIR to your school" funnel** — a single, obvious booking/request form.
- [ ] **Modern, hopeful visual refresh** — calm teal + warm coral palette, real photography, generous whitespace, soft scroll reveals. (See `/air-sandbox`.)
- [ ] **Mobile-first + accessibility pass** — WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard nav, alt text, captions. (Especially important for this audience.)
- [ ] **Core SEO hygiene** — titles, meta descriptions, one H1/page, sitemap, schema (NGO + FAQ), fast load.
- [ ] **Newsletter capture** wired to AIR's email platform.

**Stack:** rebuild on **Next.js + Tailwind + Vercel** (same proven stack as kurtisbaker.com), or — if staying on WordPress short-term — a hardened, de-cluttered theme. Recommendation: migrate off the aging WordPress/Divi build; it's the root of most QC issues.

**Deploy:** Vercel preview URL after each meaningful change.

### Tier 2 — Best-in-class (2–4 weeks)

**Goal:** A site schools forward to each other and donors trust instantly. The fundraising and growth engines come online.

- [ ] **Audience-routed homepage** — students / parents / educators / donors each find their lane in one scroll.
- [ ] **Program pages that convert** — each of the four programs gets a real page: outcomes, research, photos, testimonials, and a single CTA.
- [ ] **Recurring-giving program** — "Become a Monthly Supporter," with suggested amounts tied to impact ("$25/mo = a classroom a month").
- [ ] **Sponsor-a-school product** — a named, purchasable impact unit.
- [ ] **Impact dashboard** — live-ish counters (students reached, schools served, states), pulled from a simple CMS — donor catnip and grant-ready.
- [ ] **Stories / blog as a content engine** — student stories, parent guides, the research. Drives SEO *and* nurture.
- [ ] **CMS** (Sanity or similar) so staff/volunteers publish without a developer.
- [ ] **Email journeys** — welcome series, donor thank-you + impact loop, lapsed-donor re-engagement, school-follow-up. The nurture machine.
- [ ] **"Start the Conversation" toolkit** — gated-but-free downloads (parent guide, club starter kit, signs-and-symptoms card) as lead hooks.
- [ ] **Analytics + goals** — track donations, bookings, signups; see what converts.
- [ ] **GEO/AEO layer** — FAQ schema, an `llms.txt`, answer-first content so AI assistants recommend AIR. (See SEO/GEO section.)

### Tier 3 — Award-grade & self-sustaining (1–3 months)

**Goal:** A site that wins design awards *and* runs as a growth + fundraising machine. AIR becomes a recognized national brand in youth mental health.

- [ ] **Scroll-driven story of AIR** — Kenny's story and AIR's growth unfold cinematically (reduced-motion respected).
- [ ] **"Start an AIR Club" engine** — students launch chapters; a self-replicating growth + volunteer + future-donor network (the Active Minds lesson). This is the scale-beyond-NJ play.
- [ ] **Interactive impact map** — every school, every state, lighting up as the movement spreads.
- [ ] **Annual campaign hub** — a repeatable yearly fundraising + awareness campaign around National Suicide Prevention Day / Back-to-School.
- [ ] **Peer-to-peer & event fundraising** — supporters raise money on AIR's behalf (walks, birthdays, dog events).
- [ ] **Branded content series** — student voices, the therapy dogs, parent stories; built for social + SEO + email.
- [ ] **Custom illustration & motion language** — a brand world (the "AIR" breath metaphor), extending to social and decks.
- [ ] **Professional photo/video** — real students, real dogs, real classrooms (with consent) — the single biggest credibility upgrade.
- [ ] **Award submissions** — Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, Webby (Nonprofit), plus nonprofit-sector recognition (Classy Awards, Anthem Awards). The press from these *is* fundraising.

## SEO & GEO/AEO strategy

Two jobs: **be found by humans in search (SEO)** and **be recommended by AI assistants (GEO/AEO — Generative/Answer Engine Optimization).** Both matter now; a parent or student increasingly asks ChatGPT or Google's AI "how do I help a friend who might be suicidal" before they click a blue link.

**Technical SEO (Tier 1):**
- One canonical host; 301 everything else (kill the `host.air.ngo` split — see QC report).
- Clean titles + meta descriptions per page; exactly one H1.
- XML sitemap, robots, fast Core Web Vitals (the WordPress build likely fails these).
- Structured data: `NGO`/`NonProfit` org schema, `FAQPage`, `Event` (for conferences/presentations), `Article` (stories).

**Content SEO (Tier 2):** target the questions this audience actually searches —
- "warning signs of teen depression / suicide," "how to help a friend who is struggling," "youth mental health first aid near me," "mental health speaker for schools NJ," "emotional support dog program."
- Each becomes an answer-first article that also nurtures and converts. AIR's lived expertise + research validation is a genuine ranking advantage.

**GEO/AEO (Tier 2–3):** get AIR cited by AI assistants —
- **Answer-first writing**: lead every page/FAQ with a direct, quotable answer (the sandbox FAQ already does this).
- **`llms.txt`** describing AIR, its programs, crisis resources, and how to engage.
- **Entity clarity**: consistent NAP (name/address/phone), org schema, Wikipedia/Wikidata presence, authoritative inbound links (TCNJ study, NJAMHAA, news) so models trust and surface AIR.
- **Crisis-query safety**: ensure AIR's content always routes to 988 — being the *responsible* source is what gets you recommended on sensitive queries.

**Local/visibility:** Google Business Profile, Charity Navigator / Candid (GuideStar) profiles complete and verified (donor trust + SEO), GiveButter/Classy presence.

## Program sequencing & implementation on the site

Map each program to a clear on-site journey, owner, and conversion:

| Program | On-site job | Primary CTA | Owner | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Coming Up for AIR®** (presentations) | Make schools trust + book it | "Request a presentation" | Programs | 1–2 |
| **Youth Mental Health First Aid** | Sell/schedule trainings | "Register for a training" | Programs | 2 |
| **AIR Dogs: Paws for Minds®** | Tell the story, recruit teams, drive events | "Meet the dogs / volunteer" | Outreach | 2 |
| **Community Outreach & Conference** | Promote events, sell tickets, recap | "Attend / sponsor" | Events | 2–3 |

**Sequencing logic:** Tier 1 nails the two highest-leverage conversions (donate + book a presentation). Tier 2 builds out the full program pages and the fundraising engine. Tier 3 turns programs into *movements* (clubs, campaigns, peer fundraising).

## Measuring impact (so the board can see it working)

Define success before launch. Suggested KPIs by tier:

- **Tier 1:** site speed (Core Web Vitals green), zero broken links/duplicate pages, mobile usability 100, newsletter signups > 0.
- **Tier 2:** online donations ↑, monthly donors started, presentation requests ↑, email list growth rate, organic traffic ↑.
- **Tier 3:** clubs started, recurring revenue as % of total, returning-donor rate, branded-search volume ("Attitudes In Reverse"), awards/press earned.

**North-star metric:** *students reached per dollar raised* — the number that proves AIR is both impactful and efficient, and the one that wins grants.

## How to view the sandbox

The working prototype lives at **`/air-sandbox`** in this repo.

- **Local:** `npm install && npm run dev`, then open `http://localhost:3000/air-sandbox`.
- **Temporary live link:** every push to this branch produces a Vercel **preview deployment**; the sandbox is at `<preview-url>/air-sandbox`. Share that link with the board.
- It's a **design + messaging prototype**: copy and stats are sourced from public info and should be verified, CTAs point to placeholder destinations, and the newsletter form is inert until wired to AIR's email/CRM. It is `noindex` so it never competes with the real site in search.
